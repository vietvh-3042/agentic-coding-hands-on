## 1. Overview

**Problem:** Viewers of the Sun* Kudos board have no quick way to show appreciation for a kudo
they did not write themselves, and a sender has no lightweight signal of how well their kudo
landed with the community.
**Solution:** Every kudo card carries a heart control. A signed-in member clicks it to toggle a
heart on or off; the count updates immediately, then settles on the server's confirmed number —
worth double during an admin-configured special-day window.
**Scope:** Hearting and un-hearting any kudo except the viewer's own; showing the current heart
count and the viewer's own liked state on both the scrolling feed and the highlight carousel; the
special-day double-value window.
**Non-Scope:** Configuring the special-day window itself — no screen exists for setting it (see
§ 11 Risks & Known Issues); notifying the kudo's sender that they were hearted — no notification is
ever generated by this feature.

## 4. Requirements

### Foundation (0xx)

- **FR-001** Every kudo keeps a running heart count that always matches the number of hearts actually recorded for it.

### Sun* Kudos — Live Board (2xx)

- **FR-201** On both the scrolling feed and the highlight carousel, every kudo card shows a heart control with its current heart count and whether the viewer has already hearted it.

### Interaction (4xx)

- **FR-401** Clicking the heart control adds a heart the first time and removes that same heart on a second click — one control handles both directions.
- **FR-402** The heart control disables itself on the viewer's own kudo, and again while a click is still being processed, so rapid repeat clicks can never queue a second write.
- **FR-403** During an admin-configured special-day window, a heart is automatically worth two instead of one; the viewer's immediate guess corrects itself once the server responds.

### Security (6xx)

- **FR-601** Only a signed-in member may heart or un-heart a kudo.
- **FR-602** A member can never heart their own kudo, and may only remove a heart that they themselves placed.

## 5. Business Rules

- One heart per member per kudo; clicking again removes it rather than adding a second heart (BR-001)
- A member can never heart their own kudo — the control disables itself, and the database independently refuses the write (BR-002)
- During the special-day window, a heart grants the sender two instead of one, resolved entirely server-side (BR-003)
- Un-hearting always revokes exactly the amount that heart originally granted, even after the special-day window has closed (BR-004)
- The control decides, from its own current liked state, whether a click adds or removes a heart — one toggle, never two independent actions (DEC-001)
- The heart control's liked/unliked state moves between exactly two states, one per click (SM-001)

### User Journey

1. User arrives at the Sun* Kudos — Live Board and sees kudo cards in the feed and the highlight carousel, each with a heart icon and count.
2. User clicks the heart on a kudo that isn't their own — the heart fills in and the count goes up immediately.
3. The system confirms the reaction with its true count (doubled, on a special day); clicking the same heart again removes it.

## 7. User Stories

### US017_ReactToKudoWithHeart — React to a Kudo with a Heart

**Actor:** Signed-in Sunner
**Goal:** Heart (and un-heart) a kudo
**Business value:** Lets a member show appreciation for a kudo without having to write one of their own, and gives the sender a visible signal of how well their kudo landed

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- [ ] Clicking the heart on a kudo that isn't the viewer's own immediately flips liked/unliked with an optimistic ±1 guess, then reconciles to the server's authoritative count
- [ ] A first heart adds the reaction; clicking the same heart again removes it — the same control, not two separate controls
- [ ] The control disables itself on the viewer's own kudos and while a request is pending, so rapid repeat clicks never queue a second write
- [ ] A special-day window grants a 2x heart automatically — the optimistic guess is corrected to the real value once the server responds
- [ ] A failed write leaves the count untouched and shows an inline error

## 8. Scenarios

### US017_ReactToKudoWithHeart — Happy Path

**Given** a kudo the viewer has not yet hearted, with no special-day window active, **When** the viewer clicks the heart icon, **Then** the heart fills in, the count increases by one immediately, and settles at the server's confirmed count.

### US017_ReactToKudoWithHeart — Error: write fails

**Given** the viewer clicks the heart icon, **When** the reaction fails to save (e.g. a network error), **Then** the optimistic guess rolls back to the pre-click state and an inline error message appears.

## 9. Edge Cases

| Un-hearting a kudo that was granted a double heart during a special-day window, after the window has closed | The count still drops by the full amount that heart originally granted, not just one     | "None — silent handling"                  |
